About

This application runs entirely in your browser. Files are read locally and are not uploaded to a server.

The generated image uses the ISO 9660 filesystem and can be mounted as a CD/DVD image by compatible operating systems and virtual machines.

ISO 9660 filenames are normalized for compatibility. Very long or unusual filenames may be shortened.
